Who we are

The property company of the Nigerian Air Force

We give real value to every client, from first-time homeowners and tenants to investors at home and abroad.

Incorporated in 2011 as a subsidiary of the Nigerian Air Force Investment Limited Group, NAF Properties builds and manages estates with the discipline, security and accountability the Air Force is known for.

Residential

Secure estates for homeowners, tenants and investors, built to deliver value for money.

Commercial

Shopping complexes, offices and lease spaces in prime, high-traffic locations.

Infrastructure
