Vision, mission & values

What we stand for

Our purpose is rooted in service to the Nigerian Air Force and to every Nigerian looking for a secure home.

Company overviewLeadershipVision, mission & valuesMeet the team
Our vision

To be the prime military brand in property development, with a strong sense of corporate social responsibility, within and outside Nigeria.

Our mission

To manage NAF assets and properties, develop property through commercial partnerships, and supply building materials, for the benefit of the NAF and the general public.

Core values

Integrity, excellence and professionalism

Three values guide how we build, sell and manage property.

Core value 01

Integrity

We keep our word to residents, investors and partners, with honest, transparent dealings on every transaction.

Core value 02

Excellence

We hold every home, road and service to a high standard, and keep improving how we work.

Core value 03

Professionalism

We bring Air Force discipline, accountability and respect to every client relationship.

Our mandate

What our mission covers

  • Facility management of Nigerian Air Force assets and properties.
  • Commercial arrangements for property development, including public–private partnerships.
  • Manufacturing, importation and distribution of building materials for the construction industry.
